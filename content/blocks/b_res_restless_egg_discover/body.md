Restless EggAboutThe EggRestless Egg is the accelerator for a new class of founder, one who is native to culture and fluent in code.This emerging founder archetype will define the next generation of technology companies, particularly as models, tooling, and intelligence commoditize and the frontier shifts to taste, experience and humanity.We believe AI and emerging technologies should not only accelerate productivity, but also expand the ways in which people communicate, feel, imagine, relate, play, create, gather, and live together.For that reason we are especially interested in category defining technology products, which go beyond present day discussions about productivity, automation, or efficiency. Our OfferWe run a six-month accelerator for founders ready to turn mature ideas, prototypes, and creative practices into products, companies, and markets. We provide:- Initial investment to support the first six months of focused company-building.- Hands-on support across product development, positioning, strategy, fundraising, hiring, and go-to-market.- Weekly check-ins, monthly crits, and milestone reviews to help founders move from experimentation to traction.- Access to our network of founders, technologists, operators, subject matter experts, and early-stage investors.- Follow-on investment for companies ready to scale.Our typical structure is an initial $100k investment, with the possibility of up to $175k in follow-on funding depending on the progress and needs of the company.Our IncentivesIn return, Restless Egg takes a small equity stake in the company. We only see a financial return if the company succeeds commercially. You retain ownership of your IP, and if your product does not work out, you do not owe us anything.Our upside is tied to yours. When a founder builds something commercially successful, that success allows the model to compound. Simply put, returns from one company enable us to fund and support the next generation of founders.Our mission is to build a sustainable financial ecosystem for creative founders, one where the success of one company helps make possible many more companies, experiments, and new cultural forms over time.Why Restless Egg?Technology is a medium for creative and intuitive expression that reshapes how we engage with the world. The startup is one of the dominant forms through which that medium now reaches society. As startups once reinvented corporations, creative founders have the opportunity to reshape the startup model: what it builds, who it serves, how it feels, and what kinds of futures it makes possible.Restless Egg brings together capital, community, and company-building support to generate novel organizational structures and institutional forms that foster creativity, embrace risk, and deliver meaningful change.Our goal is to build a sustainable ecosystem for creative founders, one where creative experimentation becomes commercial product product, product can become company, and product and company—these being the media commensurate with the scale and speed of our society—become vehicles for meaningful cultural change.
