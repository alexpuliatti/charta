FROM THE MID-19TH TO THE EARLY 20TH 
CENTURY, A DYNAMIC PERIOD IN AMERICAN 
HISTORY WHEN WAVES OF IMMIGRANTS FROM 
ALL CORNERS OF THE WORLD CAME TO NEW 
YORK SEEKING OPPORTUNITY. IMMIGRATION TO 
NYC FAR OUTWEIGHED THAT IN ANY OTHER CITY 
IN THE UNITED STATES AND NEW YORK CITY’S 
CHINATOWN EVENTUALLY BECAME THE 
LARGEST CHINATOWN IN THE U.S.
